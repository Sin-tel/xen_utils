User notes:

* Do not trust your existing music theory instinct. E.g. in 22 equal temperament, D# is a major third, this is normal. Ask me for guidance if something looks off, don't assume.
* Don't rely too much on mental arithmetic. If you need to test an idea, write scratch scripts in examples. The diophantine solvers are reliable and extensively tested.
* If you have a promising idea: implement it as an example and let me inspect the output.
* Don't bother with docs or comments while we are still figuring out what the API should look like.
* Don't invent your own temperaments, check data/temperaments.txt for known good ones. 99% of temperaments are total garbage.
* Ideally, there are no empirical weights anywhere.
